LAYOUT TIPS
-----------
- Sky Blue (#E8F1FB) background
- Device mockup centered
- Primary caption in Deep Blue (#1A3A5C) above or below the device; subcaption smaller, in Slate Gray (#5A6978)
- One accent word per headline in Accent Blue (#2D7FF9)
- Keep it clean and readable at small sizes

CAPTURE (source PNGs, 1.4.0)
----------------------------
Scenes (same for every locale, light mode, portrait):
1. iPhone: search results for a typical word, keyboard hidden (el: "θαλασσα" typed without accents)
2. iPhone: Settings → Manage Dictionaries (import screen)
3. iPhone: Settings → Dictionary Order (one row per language pair, (i) buttons)
4. iPad 13": search results for the same word, keyboard hidden

Devices: iPhone 17 Pro (iOS 26.4) → screenshot-N-iphone17p-<locale>-source.png (es, ar, el);
iPhone 17 Pro Max (iOS 26.4) → screenshot-N-iphone17pm-<locale>-source.png (en, ru);
iPad Pro 13-inch (M5) (iOS 26.4) → screenshot-4-ipad13-<locale>-source.png.

Steps:
- Fresh install (xcrun simctl uninstall <udid> com.kyukhin.DictApp), launch with
  -AppleLanguages (<lang>) -AppleLocale <lang_REGION> -resetData -disableReviewPrompt
- Wait until the Search screen shows: every dictionary is searchable at once,
  nothing is unpacked (#138).
- Status bar: xcrun simctl status_bar <udid> override --time 9:41 --batteryState charged
  --batteryLevel 100 --cellularBars 4 --wifiBars 3
- On iPadOS 26 the search field sits under the title (#120): tap it before typing.
- Reject captures with clipped text, a visible keyboard or a progress indicator.
  A stray home-indicator bar is simulator state: reboot the simulator and retake.
