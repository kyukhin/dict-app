# Fixture: ell-example-not-greek

A short rule: *σπίτι* is a house.

| | Singular |
|---|---|
| Nominative | το σπίτι |

## Examples

- The house is big.
  The house is big.
- Έγραψα ένα γράμμα.
  I wrote a letter.
- Βλέπω το σπίτι.
  I see the house.
