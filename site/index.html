LibreDict

An offline dictionary that helps you learn words

LibreDict is a fast, lightweight dictionary for English, Greek, Spanish, Russian and Arabic. Look a word up, then learn it.

Dictionary

English is included; other languages download once and then work offline. Search finds any inflected form (έγραψα finds γράφω), and Greek words show declension and conjugation tables.

Learn

Word lists by level (Greek A1–B1, English A1–C2) or your own from CSV, Anki or Quizlet. Daily practice with spaced repetition and real example sentences.

Grammar

A conjugation drill and short grammar topics, with Greek as the first language.

Privacy

No ads, no accounts, no tracking. Everything stays on your device.
