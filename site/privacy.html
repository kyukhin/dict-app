Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-11-09

What the app collects

Nothing. The app has no analytics, no ads, no accounts and no identifiers. Your history, My words, learning progress and statistics stay on your device.

Language downloads

When you ask for a language, the app downloads public files from GitHub Releases. As for any download, GitHub receives your IP address and the system user agent, under GitHub's privacy statement. Nothing identifies you to LibreDict.

Report a bug

Only when you tap it. The Mail app shows you the whole message, including the app and iOS versions, before you send it.

Daily reminder

The reminder is a local notification on your device. Nothing is sent to a server.

This website

It sets no cookies, loads nothing from other servers and counts no visits.

Contact

Questions: support@libredict.app.
