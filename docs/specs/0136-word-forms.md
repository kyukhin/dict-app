# Word forms

> Spec written by Claude (AI) for LibreDict.
> Status: Implemented (approved by kyukhin on 2026-10-28, the #144 change on 2026-10-31) · Epic: #136 · Tickets: #141, #142, #143, #144, #306 · Updated: 2026-11-11

Read [0000-overview.md](0000-overview.md) and [0129-dictionary-file.md](0129-dictionary-file.md) first. This epic builds on #129's read-only `dictionaries.sqlite`. #237 ([0237-downloads.md](0237-downloads.md), Approved) moves the Greek tables (`lemma`, `tagset`, `paradigm`, `form_lookup`, `form_link`) and their `data_source` row into the Greek pack, attached as `ell`: since T2 (#241) their SQL says `ell.` where this spec says `dict.`, and without the Greek pack `ell` is the empty stub: the same tables with no rows, so the queries return nothing and need no special code. `morphy_exception` and `morphy_lemma` stay in the bundle as `dict.`. Numbers marked "measured" come from a prototype of the pipeline, run on 2026-10-27 against the 2026-10-01 Wiktionary snapshot and the 1.4.0 seed.

## Why

Greek is highly inflected. Of the 5,000 most frequent Greek words in subtitles, 38.9% are headwords of the bundled dictionaries. Most of the rest are inflected forms (έχεις, ήταν, είπε) that find nothing today. English learners hit the same wall with irregular forms (went, mice). Learners also want to see how a word inflects. This epic adds a form → lemma step to search and inflection tables to the Definition screen. The Greek forms data is the layer that word lists (#130), the conjugation drill (#132) and examples (#134) reuse later.

## Behaviour

### Search by any form (#142, #143)

- After sanitising, a one-word query of 2+ letters is also looked up as an inflected form:
  - every letter Greek: in the Greek forms table, ignoring accents, case and final sigma (`εγραψα`, `ΈΓΡΑΨΑ` = `έγραψα`);
  - every letter a–z, any case: through the WordNet morphy port, with lemma rows from dictionaries whose headwords are English only (kyukhin, 2026-11-02);
  - mixed scripts, other scripts or several words: no form step.
- The lemma's rows (its headword entries in the enabled dictionaries, never a proper noun) join the exact matches, before the prefix matches. Each carries a caption under the headword line.
- **Frequency order** (kyukhin, #306): the exact matches and the lemma rows are one group, in three steps: first the rows whose headword is spelt exactly as typed, accents included (case does not count); then the lemma rows whose lemma's table holds that exact spelling (μάτια → μάτι; English lemmas have no table); then the rest. Within each step, most frequent first by `word_rank`; rows without a rank go last, in the order of #142. `ματια` shows μάτι (eyes, "nominative plural of μάτι") before ματιά (glance), as nothing is spelt `ματια`; `ματιά` shows ματιά first, `μάτια` μάτι; `better` shows better first, then well and good. English rows use the English lists' ranks (leaves → leave before leaf). Without the pack that holds a language's ranks, the order is #142's but for the first two steps.
- If the folded query is the lemma itself, nothing is added, so results for base forms change only when a row spelt as typed moves up (`ποτέ` before πότε).
- A form shared by several lemmas shows each of them: up to 5 lemmas and 10 rows. Example: κανείς and κάνεις both fold to `κανεισ`, so the query also shows κάνω.
- In "Preferred dictionary first" mode, lemma rows go into their pair's bucket, ordered with that bucket's exact matches. The top 4 per pair is applied after the merge.
- Opening a lemma row works as today. History records the opened row's source and word (#138), i.e. the lemma, not the typed form.

### Inflection tables (#144)

- The Definition screen of a Greek headword (sources `freedict-ell-eng`, `wordnet-ell-eng`) shows one table below the definition, titled "Conjugation" or "Declension". Words without a table show nothing: indeclinables, adverbs and gaps in Wiktionary.
- **Verbs, default view** (kyukhin, 2026-10-22):
  - rows εγώ, εσύ, αυτός, εμείς, εσείς, αυτοί;
  - columns Present, Imperfect, Aorist, Future (θα γράψω), in the grammar-book order;
  - an Imperative block: rows εσύ and εσείς, columns continuous (γράφε) and simple (γράψε).
  - The main voice is active, or passive when the verb has no active present (deponents such as έρχομαι).
- **"All forms"** (kyukhin, 2026-10-22) shows the rest below the default view:
  - for the main voice: future continuous (θα γράφω), subjunctive continuous and simple (να γράφω, να γράψω), perfect (έχω γράψει), pluperfect (είχα γράψει), future perfect (θα έχω γράψει), participles (γράφοντας, έχοντας γράψει) and the aorist infinitive (γράψει);
  - then everything for the other voice, if the verb has it (γράφομαι, γραφόμουν, γράφτηκα, θα γραφτώ, …, γραμμένος).
  - The button then reads "Main forms" and collapses again. Its state is not stored.
- **Nouns**: case (nominative, genitive, accusative, vocative) × number. **Adjectives**: case × gender, one block for the singular and one for the plural, then a line with the comparative and superlative when Wiktionary has them.
- **Cells**:
  - several forms in one cell are joined with " / " (γράφτηκα / γράφηκα); where the grid would not fit otherwise, each form goes on its own line after the slash;
  - forms tagged rare, archaic or dated are hidden from tables but stay searchable;
  - empty rows and columns are dropped (είμαι has no aorist, πρέπει has only αυτός);
  - a single missing cell shows "—".
- **Which table** (kyukhin, 2026-10-31 and 2026-11-01): a lemma matches the entry's word when it is spelled the same (NFC), or near: the same but for case or a diaeresis (Ταύρος and ταύρος, Μάϊος and Μάιος; a word in capitals carries no accent, so ΕΛΙΑ is ελιά). A lemma whose accent differs is another word and never matches (Σόφια is not σοφία, αλκή not άλκη, Καμπανία not καμπάνια). A lemma "has a table" when it has cells that fit a slot, or when it is an alternative or form of a lemma that has (πάω → πηγαίνω); that table then shows with the lemma under the title.
  - Map each comma-separated label of the entry's `pos`: noun → noun, adjective or adjective satellite → adj, verb → verb, proper noun → name. Other labels are skipped.
  - A lemma spelled as the headword comes first, of any of the entry's labels, a noun label also taking the proper noun: WordNet's noun Κίνα shows Κίνα, not κίνα; Γεωργία, Άγκυρα likewise. Λάος shows nothing, as its proper noun has no table and λαός is another word.
  - Otherwise try the labels in the entry's own order and take the first whose near lemmas have a table: FreeDict's καλός, "adjective, noun", shows the adjective's. If that label's lemmas have more than one table, no table is shown.
  - A noun label with no noun lemma takes the proper-noun lemmas by the same rule: WordNet labels proper nouns "noun" (Αθήνα, Αγγλία, Απρίλιος). Only a capitalized word takes a near one: the common noun ρώμη (vigour) does not show Ρώμη.
  - A label with no lemma of its part of speech at all (Wiktionary has the word only as a form or an alternative page) shows the table of the one lemma of that part of speech the word is a form or an alternative of, with that lemma under the title: μέρα → ημέρα, αγαπώ → αγαπάω, αρέσει → αρέσω. With more than one such lemma, none, as for πάω's link. The proper noun comes first: Κάιρο shows Κάιρο, not καιρός.
  - Either link counts only when the lemma's table holds the word itself, or no form that folds like it (the link then comes from the word's own page, as μέρα's). A form that only folds alike belongs to another word: μετρό does not show μέτρο, συνεδρία not συνέδριο (συνέδρια), Ινδία not ίνδιο.
  - And either link needs the word itself (kyukhin, 2026-11-01, #143): a cell of the lemma's table (πάω, αγαπώ) or the own spelling of its link page (`form_link`: μέρα, φράκτης → φράχτης). A word that only folds like a link page links nowhere: στύλος (pillar) does not show στυλό's table, βολέ not βώλος's, άριος not αραιός's, Α not άρθρο's.
  - For a noun, an adjective or a proper noun, a link whose table holds the word counts only when the word is one of its nominatives: πράγματα shows πράγμα's table, but καφέ (café) does not show καφές's, nor μικρό the noun μικρός's, nor κολιέ κολιός's. An oblique headword loses its table with them (κόμματος, κτιρίων, περίμετρο, τίγρης). Verbs link as before.
  - An entry without a `pos` (3,655 FreeDict rows) shows the table when exactly one lemma of verb, noun, adj or name has one, the lemmas spelled as the headword first (άδωρος); with more than one, none.
- **Layout**: the full grid when it fits (iPad, landscape), two tenses per band when that fits, otherwise one tense per block (narrow screens, accessibility text sizes).
- **Arabic**: the table is mirrored, so the pronoun column is on the right. Greek forms still read left to right.
- **VoiceOver** reads each cell as "column, row, form": "aorist, εγώ, έγραψα", "genitive, singular, σπιτιού", "aorist, εγώ, no form". Greek parts are spoken with the Greek voice.
- A footer under the table reads "Forms: Wiktionary, CC BY-SA 4.0".

### Decisions on record

- 2026-10-22 (kyukhin): forms for all Greek lemmas (~26k, ~23 MB), not only the most frequent.
- 2026-10-22 (kyukhin): verb tables show the main forms by default; "All forms" shows the passive, να-forms, perfect, pluperfect, future perfect and participles.
- 2026-10-22 (kyukhin, #143): English uses a Swift port of WordNet morphy plus WordNet's exception lists, not a Wiktionary English table.
- 2026-10-28 (kyukhin): the verb table's main columns follow grammar books: Present, Imperfect, Aorist, Future. "Future" is future simple (θα γράψω); future continuous (θα γράφω) is under "All forms".
- 2026-10-28 (kyukhin): an English irregular form is captioned "form of go", because morphy does not tell the past from the participle.
- 2026-11-01 (kyukhin, #143): Morphy orders its candidates by part of speech first (verb, noun, adj, adv), with exceptions before rules within each part of speech, so goes reads "form of go", not "plural of go".
- 2026-11-01 (kyukhin, #143): the dictionary file keeps a link page's own spelling (`form_link`), so a word that only folds like a link page (στύλος like στυλός, βολέ like βόλε) does not borrow its table, and a gloss that names a synonym ("a formal synonym of τσαγκάρης") is no form link. Found by #144's QA on the shipped file.
- 2026-11-02 (kyukhin, #143): English lemma rows come only from dictionaries whose headwords are English (`dict_metadata.from_lang` eng; an import once it records its language, #208). Spanish WordNet's do, idea and doctor no longer read as forms of doing, ideas and doctors (77 of the English top 5,000).
- 2026-11-02 (kyukhin, #143): within a part of speech without an exception, Morphy takes only the first rule whose base is a lemma of that part of speech, as NLTK's `morphy()` does: hoping → hope, not hop. A word that is itself a lemma keeps its rule base (things → thing, also boss → bos).
- 2026-11-02 (kyukhin, #143): a Spanish query of a–z letters keeps its English rows (planes → plane); the step does not guess the query's language.
- 2026-11-11 (kyukhin, #306): the exact matches and the lemma rows are one group: the headword spelt as typed, accents included, first; then the lemmas whose table holds that spelling; then the rest; each step by `word_rank`, unranked last. English ranks every row by its lowercased headword, whatever its source, so Spanish results keep their order; proper nouns are never lemma rows. Frequency for prefix matches and ranks for Spanish, Russian and Arabic are out of scope.
- Interface languages en, ru, es, ar (RTL), el; Wiktionary is credited (#136).
- 2026-10-31 (kyukhin, #144): an entry's table follows the order of the labels in its own `pos`, the first whose lemma has a table, instead of the fixed verb, noun, adj, name order. FreeDict's καλός ("adjective, noun") shows the adjective's table.
- 2026-10-31 (kyukhin, #144): an entry without a `pos` shows the table when its lemma, by the same word or folded-word rule, has exactly one table across all parts of speech; with more than one, none.
- 2026-10-31 (kyukhin, #144): VoiceOver keeps reading the shared Russian abbreviations (ед. ч., м. р.) as they are; a follow-up ticket after the release revisits them.
- 2026-10-31 (kyukhin, #144): when a grid does not fit, a cell's variants go one per line after the slash (γράφουν /, γράφουνε), as built.
- 2026-10-31 (kyukhin, #144): a headword Wiktionary has only as a form or an alternative page (μέρα, αρέσει, αγαπώ) shows the table of the single lemma of its part of speech it belongs to, as πάω shows πηγαίνω's; with more than one candidate, no table.
- 2026-10-31 (kyukhin, #144): a "noun" entry with no noun lemma falls back to the proper-noun lemma, since WordNet labels proper nouns "noun" (Αθήνα).
- 2026-11-01 (kyukhin, #144): a form link counts only when the headword is a nominative of the linked lemma; verbs keep the link rule as it was. Wrong links go (καφέ → καφές, μικρό → μικρός, κολιέ → κολιός, πόλο → πόλος, χημικό → χημικός, σάκε → σάκος, χούμους → χούμος), and some right ones with them (κόμματος, κτιρίων, περίμετρο, τίγρης).
- 2026-11-01 (kyukhin, #144): a lemma spelled exactly as the headword always beats one matched only by folding, across the parts of speech the entry allows, the proper noun included for a noun label (Λάος, Σόφια, Καμπανία, Μαλάκια, Γεωργία, Άγκυρα, αλκή, Κίνα). Case-only matches with no exact lemma stay (Ταύρος, Ελληνικά).
- 2026-11-02 (kyukhin, #144): a lemma whose accent differs never matches, so Σόφια, Καμπανία, Μαλάκια and αλκή show no table. Spelling variants of one word that differ in accent lose theirs too (κελτικός, στέρεος, γειτονία): a wrong table is worse than none.
- 2026-10-31 (kyukhin, #144): the large-text layout stays as QA built it (each row label above its forms); #226 tracks a designed layout after the release.

### Out of scope

Russian and Spanish forms. English inflection tables. Articles in noun tables. Highlighting the searched cell. Tables for imported dictionaries. Tables for pronouns, articles and numerals: their forms are searchable, but they get no table. Hand-written paradigms for Wiktionary gaps: gaps are fixed upstream in Wiktionary, and the next snapshot picks them up.

### Data

These tables live in `dictionaries.sqlite`. Size budgets: Greek tables ≤ 25 MB (24.3 MB as built by #143, 1.7 MB of it the two `lemma` indexes), morphy ≤ 1.5 MB (1.3 MB as built by #143). Gzip compresses them to 6.7 MB and 0.6 MB, roughly the growth of the App Store download. The build prints each table's size (`dbstat`), as #129 requires.

- Every lookup goes through the primary-key prefix `form_norm = ?`. In the prototype, a lookup that missed the key's first column scanned the table and made search 6× slower. #129 runs no `ANALYZE`, so the plan stays the primary key.
- Periphrastic forms (θα, να, έχω, είχα, έχοντας + form) are generated by the app (see "Tables"). They are never stored.

### Pipeline: Greek forms (#141)

**Source and pin**
- Input: kaikki.org's raw Wiktextract dump of the English Wiktionary, `https://kaikki.org/dictionary/raw-wiktextract-data.jsonl.gz`. The per-language files are marked deprecated. Current dump: enwiktionary 2026-10-01, 3,082,185,963 bytes, SHA-256 `784574cc…ec29b2d`. Streaming it took 12 min (11,121,531 lines).
- The build keeps lines whose `lang_code` is `el`: 85,425 entries, the same tables as the per-language file (measured).
- kaikki.org keeps only its latest dump, so a URL plus hash cannot pin it. The filtered subset is the pin: `Scripts/data/kaikki-el-20261001.jsonl.gz`, 19.1 MB in Git LFS, added once per snapshot (a dictionary rebuild adds ~130 MB). `Scripts/inputs.json` records the dump URL, date and SHA-256 together with the subset's own SHA-256, and the step checks the subset before use. The step works offline.
- `build_forms.py --refresh` streams the current dump and writes a new dated subset and its `inputs.json` entry. A refresh is a reviewed PR of its own.

**Steps** (`build(conn, cache_dir)` in `Scripts/build_forms.py`; it imports `normalize_greek` from [build_greek.py](../../Scripts/build_greek.py) and has no folding of its own)
1. **Lemma pages.** An entry is a lemma when at least one sense has no `form_of`/`alt_of` and no "… of X" gloss. A sense about a periphrastic form ("θα κάνεις: second-person singular perfective future of κάνω") is such a gloss and adds no link. One lemma per (word, pos), ordered by (word, pos), so ids are stable for a given snapshot.
2. **Cells.** Keep table cells, i.e. forms with a `source`. First:
   - a cell with variants joined by " - " (contracted verbs: `θυμάμαι - θυμούμαι`, `οδηγώ - οδηγάω`) gives one cell per variant: 1,088 cells in 63 lemmas;
   - a trailing parenthesised note (`κάνω (κάμνω →)`, `μιλάς (μιλείς → ομιλώ)`) and footnote marks (`έμπαινε²`, `θέλουν² (θένε)`) are stripped: 131 cells in 96 lemmas. A cell that is only a note stays multi-word.

   Then drop:
   - meta rows (`romanization`, `table-tags`, `inflection-template`, `class`);
   - placeholders and non-Greek strings (`-`, `‑η`, Latin letters such as `Αναξιμένoυς`, `➤`): 10,687 cells;
   - multi-word cells (Wiktionary's partial `θα γράφω` rows and prose): 11,835;
   - spellings no Greek word has, left by template errors: two accents (`γόνιμός`, `εκστρατεύόμαστε`; acute, grave and circumflex count, breathings and the diaeresis do not), 341 cells in 40 lemmas; a final sigma inside the word (`άφυλλοςος`, the whole word given as the stem), 367 in 15;
   - cells tagged `error-*` (220) or single-word `future` (315).
   No length filter: `δω` (dependent of βλέπω) is real.
3. **Table guards.**
   - Pronoun tables that mix persons are dropped. They are Wiktionary's shared personal-pronoun template, which made `σας → τα`.
   - **Another word's table.** A table none of whose cells is the headword or begins with its stem (the folded headword without its last two letters, four for -μαι: θυμάμαι → θυμ) is dropped. Wiktionary declined another word there (κρεατομηχανή held μηχανή's forms, υπερβασίλειο αυτοκρατορία's, κολοβός αναληπτικός's) or gave the template no stem (αισώπειος: ος, α, ου): 40 tables, 571 cells, all listed in the QA report. A suppletive table holds its headword, so it stays (βλέπω: είδα, δω).
   - **Bare endings.** A cell that is exactly the ending other tables give the same slot (the same tags) after their stem, and does not begin with its headword's first letter, is dropped: σαλπάρω (εί, ούν, ηκα, όμαστε), διαβαίνω (ανε), αρχαΐζων (ούσης, όντων), αιθεροβάμων (όνων), συνθέτω (όμαστε): 37 cells in 5 lemmas. A stem of one letter or none is not checked, as its forms are almost endings themselves (είμαι: όντας); nor is a form that begins like its headword (αφήνω: άσε).
   - Checked by hand: no top-500 verb, noun or adjective loses a table; the suppletive forms stay (πήγα, είδα, δω, είπα, έφαγα, ήρθα, έλα, βγήκα, μπήκα, ανέβηκα, ήπια).
4. **Links.** These turn non-lemma pages into lookup rows with their sense tags:
   - `form_of` (έγραψα → γράφω);
   - `alt_of` (αγαπώ → αγαπάω, tag `alternative`);
   - a gloss regex for pages without markup, "first-person singular dependent active of πηγαίνω and πάω" (461 links); its words are mapped to tags, and unknown words are dropped. A gloss that names a synonym ("a formal synonym of τσαγκάρης", whose "formal" holds the grammar word "form") is a meaning, not a link (#143): 4 pages (παχύς, ψωμάς, υποδηματοποιός, σιδερίτης), so υποδηματοποιός and ψωμάς are lemmas with their own tables. "A more formal variant of μιλάω" stays a link.
   Targets that are themselves form pages are followed for up to 2 steps. Multi-word forms and lemmas never enter `form_lookup`. A `form_of` or `alt_of` sense tagged `error-*` or single-word `future` gives no link, as such cells give no row (γάτος → γάτα, `error-lua-exec`; πέρδεσαι, "present/future of πέρδομαι"): 26 senses. A link without an aspect tag takes the one its gloss names ("simple past" → perfective, "imperfect" → imperfective: μετέθεσα → μεταθέτω is perfective, as #142 captions need): 5,232 links.
5. **Stoplist.** A curated list in the script (articles, conjunctions, particles, prepositions, clitic pronouns: ο, η, το, του, της, τον, αν, όταν, είτε, και, να, θα, ας, δεν, μην, σε, με, για, από, μου, σου, …) is compared exactly (NFC, lowercase, never folded). A stoplisted form is dropped from any verb, noun, adj or adv paradigm or link unless it is that lemma's own headword. It runs before the bare-ending check. Measured: 17 rows (ο@καλύπτω, αν@διαβαίνω, όταν/είτε@σαλπάρω, θα→έχω, …); the allowlist re-admits 4 of them, so 13 are dropped. αισώπειος's 7 went with its table (step 3).
   - `Scripts/data/forms-allowlist.tsv` re-admits reviewed false hits, e.g. `παρά` as a form of παράς.
   - Folded matching is wrong here: it would block κάνεις → κάνω (κανείς) and έλα → έρχομαι.
6. **Lookup.** `form_norm = normalize_greek(form)` for every kept cell, head form and link. The first cell in table order sets the caption tagset; a key with no cell takes the first link's tags, and a head form alone takes the empty tag set.
   - **Link spellings** (#143): every kept link whose page is no cell of its lemma's table also goes into `form_link` as (page word, lemma). `form_lookup` holds a link by its folded key only, so a word that folds alike (στύλος, pillar, like στυλός, the alternative form of στυλό) could not be told from the link page. A page that is a cell needs no row: `paradigm` holds its spelling.
7. **Credit.** Write the `data_source` row (see Data). Every insert runs in sorted order and nothing stores a timestamp, so the file stays byte-identical across builds (#129).

### Morphy (#143)

- **Data**: the `build_morphy.py` step reads WordNet 3.0 (`index.*` and `*.exc`) from the NLTK corpus the dictionary build already downloads and #140 pins. Same license as `wordnet`. `morphy_lemma` is needed because the `wordnet` rows' `pos` column comes from NLTK synsets and already lists morphy's guesses (`running` shows "verb"). Checking candidates against any headword added 170 wrong bases in the English top 5,000 (news → new, mother → moth).
- **Algorithm** (`Morphy`, pure Swift, the WordNet 3.0 morphy):
  0. Lowercase the token (`lowercased()`, which ignores the locale).
  1. For each pos in the order verb, noun, adj, adv: all its exception candidates from `morphy_exception` if it has any (went → go; better → good, well), else the first detachment rule hit, in this order, whose base `morphy_lemma` has for that pos (hoping → hope, not hop; kyukhin, 2026-11-02):
     - noun: s→, ses→s, xes→x, zes→z, ches→ch, shes→sh, men→man, ies→y;
     - verb: s→, ies→y, es→e, es→, ed→e, ed→, ing→e, ing→;
     - adj: er→, est→, er→e, est→e.
  2. Drop the query itself and repeated bases: a base keeps its first pos. goes is the verb go's form (es→), though `noun.exc` lists it as the plural of the noun go (kyukhin, 2026-11-01).
- **Noise guard**: rule results for nouns and adjectives need a base of 3+ letters. This removes was → wa, as → a, us → u, has → ha and keeps goes → go. Measured on the English top 5,000: 1,354 tokens get a base, 209 of them from exceptions.
- **Caption**: noun → "plural"; adj/adv ending in -er → "comparative", in -est → "superlative"; verb ending in -ing → "-ing form"; everything else → "form" ("form of go"). Morphy does not tell the past from the participle (Decisions on record).

### Search path (#142, #143)

The change goes into `DatabaseService.search` and `searchPreferred` as #138 leaves them, inside the same read:

1. **Base query.** #138's query with the same ranking and one more output column, `is_exact`: the existing `word_normalized = key COLLATE NOCASE` test, which today is only an ORDER BY term.
2. **Candidates.**
   - Greek: `SELECT … FROM dict.form_lookup JOIN dict.lemma JOIN dict.tagset WHERE form_norm = Search.foldGreek(token)`, minus lemmas whose `word_normalized` equals the key, ordered verb, noun, adj, others, then by word. No cap here: the lemma-row query's LIMIT 10 applies after the enabled-source filter.
   - English: one query on `dict.morphy_exception` and one on `dict.morphy_lemma` (an `IN` list of the rule outputs), then `Morphy`.
3. **Lemma rows.** One query of the base query's shape: an FTS column filter `{word_normalized} : ("γραφω" OR …)` plus `word_normalized COLLATE NOCASE IN (…)` over `dict.entries_fts`, with #138's imported branch when an imported source is enabled. Imported rows keep `word_normalized = word`, so that branch also matches each lemma's own word (γράφω as well as γραφω). Enabled sources only, and for English only those whose headwords are English (`dict.dict_metadata.from_lang` or `imported_sources.from_lang` = eng, `Search.englishLemmaSources`); never a proper noun (`pos IS NOT 'proper noun'`, `DatabaseService.properNounPOS`: FreeDict El–En and En–Es label them so, WordNet labels them noun; #306); LIMIT 10, ordered by candidate, then rank. The column filter uses the FTS index; a plain `word_normalized = ?` would scan, because the column has no index.
4. **Merge** with the pure `Search.mergeLemmaBucket(ranked:lemmaRows:limit:order:)`: the head group (exact rows, then lemma rows not already present), sorted by `order` when the query has a form step (#306), then the remaining base rows, cut to `limit`.
   - **Order** (#306), `Search.HeadOrder`, pure: a stable sort of the head group by (tier, rank). The typed spelling is the sanitized query, lowercased NFC, with a word-final σ after a letter as ς (`lowercased()` makes every Σ σ, so ΚΑΛΟΎΣ = καλούς), as headwords and tables spell it. Tier 0: the row's headword, lowercased NFC, equals it. Tier 1: a lemma row whose lemma's table holds it (`typedLemmas`; English lemma rows have no table). Tier 2: the rest. Rank: the row's headword key in `word_rank` (0130, "List data"): `ell` with the folded headword for a Greek step, `eng` with the lowercased headword for an English step, whatever the row's source: a Spanish row spelt as the query shares the exact rows' key, so they tie and keep #142's order (QA: no, color, hotel). No rank sorts after every rank; ties keep #142's order.
   - **Its reads**, in the same read as the search: one query for the ranks of the head groups' distinct keys plus the query's own key (every exact row's key, so a preferred-mode tail is covered too), on the primary key of the language's lists file (`DatabaseService.headRanksSQL`, `DictionarySchemas.listsSchema`: `ell` for Greek, `eng_lists` for English). Without that pack the stub's empty table gives no rank. For a Greek query with lemma rows, one more: the lemmas whose table holds the typed spelling (`greekFormLemmasSQL`). In preferred mode every bucket's base query runs first, so one rank query covers all buckets.
   - Relevance mode: the limit is 50.
   - Preferred mode: one merge per pair bucket with `topN`, before the tail query, so the tail excludes the merged ids. `Search.assemble` then works as today.
   - Lemma rows from sources outside every pair group (imports not yet in the order) are merged into the relevance tail the same way, with the tail's limit.
5. **Result type.** `SearchResult { entry: DictionaryEntry; formMatch: FormMatch? }` replaces `[DictionaryEntry]` in `SearchViewModel.results`; its identity is the entry id, unique across both stores (#129). `FormMatch` holds the lemma word and either a tag set (Greek) or a morphy pos, rule and suffix (English). Nothing of it is persisted.

**Caption.** `FormCaption.text(for:locale:)` is pure. It resolves its strings through the per-locale bundle, as `SettingsViewModel.localizedString(_:locale:)` does.
- Greek labels, joined with spaces:
  - finite verbs: tense or mood (present, imperfect, aorist, dependent, imperative continuous/simple), then "passive" if tagged, then person-number (1sg … 3pl);
  - nonfinite: participle or aorist infinitive;
  - nominals: comparative/superlative if tagged, then case, number, and gender for adjectives;
  - `alternative` → "alternative form"; nothing recognised → "form".
- The result goes into the format `forms.caption` ("%1$@ of %2$@").
- In Arabic the caption starts with U+200F, and the Greek lemma is wrapped in FSI…PDI, as in #119.
- VoiceOver reads the person as the Greek pronoun ("aorist, εγώ of γράφω"), the same words as in the tables.

**Performance.** #306 adds one primary-key query per form-step search, and one more for a Greek query with lemma rows (both in `testRankLookupUsesPrimaryKey`). Measured on the 1.4.0 seed with the prototype tables attached as a second schema (#129's layout), 17 probes, median of 5 runs: 33.1 ms without the lemma step, 31.8 ms with it, i.e. within noise. Prefix keystrokes cost one primary-key probe that finds nothing. Gate: on #129's probe set plus the inflected probes, search with the lemma step is at most 10% slower than the 1.4.0 baseline, measured as #129 measures (iPhone 15 Pro simulator, iOS 17.5, median of 3, and a device).

### Tables (#144)

- **Loading.** `DatabaseService.inflection(for: DictionaryEntry) -> LemmaParadigm?` reads the candidate lemmas (`word_normalized` index) and returns the cells (form plus tag set) of the table the "Which table" rule picks. The rule is the pure `InflectionLookup.choose`: it sorts the candidates by `InflectionLookup.match` (exact, near, or none) and asks for each matching lemma's table (its own cells, else its link's) and, for a label without a lemma, for the table of the word's single link of that part of speech. A link needs the word as a cell of its table or as its link page's spelling (`form_link`, in `linkedLemmasSQL`, #143); it is dropped when `InflectionLookup.linkFits` finds that its table only holds another word folded alike, or, for a nominal, holds the word in no nominative; a table is kept only if `ParadigmLayout.hasTable`. `DefinitionViewModel` publishes it. Entries of other sources skip the query.
- **Slots.** `ParadigmLayout` is pure. It maps cells to slots by tag subsets:
  - voice: `passive` → passive, otherwise active;
  - persons: first/second/third-person × singular/plural;
  - present: `present`, indicative or with no mood (the present-only template of πρόκειται, αφορά, οφείλω tags none), not `dependent`;
  - imperfect: `imperfect`;
  - aorist: `past perfective`;
  - dependent: `dependent`;
  - imperative continuous/simple: `imperative` with `imperfective`/`perfective`, 2sg and 2pl;
  - aorist infinitive: `infinitive-aorist`;
  - participles: `participle` with `present`/`past`, nominative masculine singular only;
  - nominals: case × number (× gender);
  - adjective degrees: `comparative` and `superlative`.
  Cells tagged `rare`, `archaic` or `dated` are hidden; cells matching no slot are not shown.
- **Generated forms**, one per variant of the source cell:
  - future continuous: θα + present; future simple: θα + dependent;
  - subjunctive: να + present (continuous), να + dependent (simple);
  - perfect: έχω/έχεις/έχει/έχουμε/έχετε/έχουν + aorist infinitive of the same voice;
  - pluperfect: είχα/είχες/είχε/είχαμε/είχατε/είχαν + the same infinitive;
  - future perfect: θα + the perfect;
  - perfect participle: έχοντας + infinitive.
  The auxiliaries are constants in code: fixed grammar, not data.
- **Model.** `InflectionTable` → sections (title, `isMain`) → columns and rows → cells (forms, accessibility label, id). Sections, columns and rows that end up empty are dropped. Both voices of a verb have the same sections: Present, Imperfect, Aorist, Future; the imperative; future continuous, perfect, pluperfect, future perfect; the subjunctive; participles and the infinitive. The first two of the main voice are the default view; the other voice starts with a voice heading.
- **Views.** `InflectionTableView` uses `ViewThatFits(in: .horizontal)` over three renderings of the same model: a full `Grid`, bands of two columns, and one block per column. When a block does not fit either (accessibility text sizes), each row label goes above its forms and every text wraps, so the screen never scrolls sideways. Each data cell is one accessibility element with the composed label, and header cells are hidden from VoiceOver. The section title has the `.isHeader` trait. Greek runs carry the `el` language attribute. Layout uses leading/trailing only, so RTL mirrors itself.
- **Pronouns** (εγώ, εσύ, αυτός, εμείς, εσείς, αυτοί) are Greek constants and are not translated.
- **Identifiers**: `inflection_table`, `inflection_all_forms_button`, cells `inflection_cell_<voice>_<slot>_<person>` (e.g. `inflection_cell_active_aorist_1sg`) or `inflection_cell_<case>_<number>[_<gender>]`, `form_caption` on the Search row, `credits_wiktionary` (and `settings_credits_link`, the Credits row in Settings).

**New catalog keys** (about 45, all 5 languages, written by the implementing agent; `testEveryCatalogKeyIsTranslatedInEveryShippedLanguage` covers them):
- `forms.section.conjugation`, `forms.section.declension`, `forms.section.nonfinite`, `forms.button.allForms`, `forms.button.mainForms`, `forms.source`, `forms.caption`;
- tenses: present, imperfect, aorist, future, futureContinuous, dependent, perfect, pluperfect, futurePerfect;
- moods and aspects: subjunctive, imperative, continuous, simple;
- voices: active, passive;
- nonfinite: participle, infinitive; the table rows present, past and perfect participle (#144);
- 4 cases, 2 numbers, 3 genders, 2 degrees, 6 person-number abbreviations;
- `forms.alternative`, `forms.form`, `forms.ingForm`, `forms.a11y.noForm`;
- `credits.dataSources.wiktionary`.
