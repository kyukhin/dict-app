## Description

LibreDict is a fast, lightweight dictionary that also helps you learn words. The English dictionary is included. Greek, Spanish, Russian and Arabic download once: you choose them and see the sizes first. The English word lists download when you first need them. Then everything works offline.

Learn:
- A tab for each language you learn, with its own lessons, lists, grammar and statistics. Greek and English to start: Greek through English or Russian, English in English or through Spanish or Greek. Greek via Russian is limited for now: words are practised in English.
- Daily practice with spaced repetition: exercises, match the pairs and a round for your mistakes. Leave a lesson and continue it later.
- A wrong answer also shows what your choice means.
- Word lists by level: Greek A1 to B1, English A1 to C2 from CEFR-J and Octanove. Import your own from CSV, TSV, Anki or Quizlet.
- Conjugation drill and grammar topics, Greek first: verbs drilled to the official Greek exam syllabus, and 17 A1 topics explained in English or Russian. Words link to their topics.
- Example sentences from Tatoeba, with translations.
- Statistics, streaks, playful sounds and an optional daily reminder.

Look up:
- Search finds inflected forms: type έγραψα and get γράφω, type went and get go. Greek words show their conjugation and declension tables.
- Dictionaries: English (WordNet), Russian, Spanish, Arabic and Greek, 400,000+ words in all.
- Type Greek without accents or Arabic without diacritics and still find the word.
- Color-coded results, your own dictionary order, and one-tap dictionary chips under the search field.
- Import your own dictionaries from .json or .sqlite files.
- Five app languages: English, Russian, Spanish, Arabic and Greek, with full right-to-left layout for Arabic.

Privacy: no ads, no accounts, no tracking, nothing collected. Language downloads are plain downloads of public files from GitHub, which receives your IP address and the system user agent, as for any download.

LibreDict stays out of your way: just you and the words.

## App Review Notes

Languages other than English download from GitHub on first launch or in Settings → Dictionaries → Languages. No account. The daily reminder is an optional local notification that the user turns on in Settings → Learning; nothing is sent to a server. Learning sounds play only while practising and follow the Sounds switch in Settings.
